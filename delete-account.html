Delete your Class Manager account and data

Class Manager: Teacher Toolkit (Android app io.github.mrwengkb.classmanager and the website mrwengkb.github.io/ClassManager) · Last updated: 24 November 2026

You sign in to Class Manager with your Google account. Class Manager has no server of its own and no separate account: your class data is saved only in a “Class Manager” folder in your own Google Drive and on your device. The developer never receives a copy, so you can delete everything yourself, at any time, with the steps below.

Steps to delete your account and all your data

  1. Delete your class files. Open drive.google.com, find the folder named “Class Manager”, and delete it. Then open Trash in Google Drive and delete it from there too (or choose “Empty trash”) to remove it immediately.
  2. Remove Class Manager's access to your Google account. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Class Manager, and choose Delete all connections (or “Remove access”). This is what closes your Class Manager account: the app can no longer sign in as you or reach your Drive.
  3. Remove the data saved on your device.
    • Android app: uninstall Class Manager, or go to Settings → Apps → Class Manager → Storage → Clear storage.
    • Website: in the app tap Sign out, then clear the site data for mrwengkb.github.io in your browser settings.
  4. Optional: reset your advertising ID. On Android go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

Need help? Open an issue at github.com/MrWengKB/ClassManager/issues. Please do not post student names or other personal details there.

What is deleted

  • Class data (class lists and student names, seating plans, groups, roles, points, reading logs and settings) stored in the “Class Manager” folder in your Google Drive. Deleted as soon as you remove the folder and empty the Drive trash.
  • Student view file (if you turned the student view on), which is in the same folder. Deleting it makes all student QR cards and links stop working.
  • Your Google name and email and the sign-in token kept on your device. Deleted when you clear the app's storage or the site data, and the token stops working when you remove access in step 2.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing is kept by the developer. Class Manager has no server and no database, so there is no copy for us to delete or retain.
  • Google Drive trash: if you delete the folder but do not empty the trash, Google keeps it in Drive trash for up to 30 days and then deletes it.
  • Ads and statistics: if you watched an optional support ad, Google AdMob may have collected your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data. The website and app also use Google Analytics to count visits. This data is handled and retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's data retention policy; it never includes your class data or student names and is not available to the developer in a form linked to you. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID (step 4) disconnects it from future ads.

Delete only some data, without deleting your account

You can also delete part of your data and keep using Class Manager:

  • In the app, remove single students, clear a whole class list, delete point items, roles and seating layouts. Changes are saved to your Drive straight away.
  • Turn off the student view for a class to delete its shared student file.
  • In Google Drive, delete individual class files inside the “Class Manager” folder.

See also the Privacy Policy.

删除课堂管家账号和资料

课堂管家没有自己的服务器，资料只保存在你自己 Google Drive 的「Class Manager」文件夹和你的设备上。要全部删除：

  1. 在 Google Drive 删除「Class Manager」文件夹，并清空回收站（不清空的话，Google 会在 30 天后自动删除）。
  2. 到 myaccount.google.com/permissions 选择 Class Manager，删除所有连接。
  3. 卸载安卓应用或在「设置 → 应用 → Class Manager → 存储」清除存储；网页版请退出登录并清除本网站的浏览器资料。
  4. （可选）在「设置 → Google → 广告」重设或删除广告 ID。

开发者不保留任何资料。AdMob 和 Google Analytics 的资料由 Google 按其政策保存，不包含班级资料或学生名字。你也可以只删除部分资料：在应用里删除学生、名单、加分项目、职务，关闭学生端，或在 Drive 里删除个别班级文件。

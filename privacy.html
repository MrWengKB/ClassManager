Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Class Manager is a free classroom tool that runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server of its own and does not keep a copy of your data.

What we access

  • Your Google account email and name, only to show who is signed in on the page. They are kept in your browser, not sent anywhere else.
  • Google Drive, limited to files Class Manager creates (the drive.file permission). Your class layouts, class lists, seating plans and roles are saved as files in a folder called “Class Manager” in your own Google Drive. Class Manager cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

Your class data is stored in your Google Drive and in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can view, share, download or delete these files in Google Drive at any time.

Website statistics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. This uses cookies and does not include your class data, student names or Google Drive files.

Removing access

You can remove Class Manager's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your data, delete the “Class Manager” folder in your Google Drive and clear this site's data in your browser.

Use of Google user data

Class Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to save and load your own classes, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Contact

Questions or requests: please open an issue at github.com/MrWengKB/ClassManager/issues.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 22 日

Class Manager 是一个免费的课堂工具，完全在你的浏览器里运行。它没有自己的服务器，也不会保留你的资料副本。

我们会使用的资料

  • 你的 Google 账号 email 和名字：只用来在页面上显示谁已登录，保存在你的浏览器里，不会传到别处。
  • Google Drive，但仅限 Class Manager 自己建立的文件（drive.file 权限）：你的教室布局、名单、座位安排和职务，会保存在你自己 Google Drive 的「Class Manager」文件夹里。Class Manager 看不到、也不能修改你 Drive 里的其他文件。

资料存放在哪里

班级资料只存放在你的 Google Drive，以及你自己设备上浏览器的本地存储里，不会传给开发者或任何人。你可以随时在 Google Drive 查看、分享、下载或删除这些文件。

网站统计

本网站使用 Google Analytics 统计访问次数和页面使用情况。这会用到 cookie，但不包含你的班级资料、学生名字或 Google Drive 文件。

取消授权

你可以随时在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 取消 Class Manager 对你 Google 账号的访问权限。要删除资料，请删除 Google Drive 里的「Class Manager」文件夹，并清除浏览器里本网站的资料。

Google 用户资料的使用

Class Manager 对 Google API 所得资料的使用，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 要求。资料只用于保存和读取你自己的班级，绝不出售、分享或用于广告。

联系

如有问题或要求，请到 github.com/MrWengKB/ClassManager/issues 提出。
